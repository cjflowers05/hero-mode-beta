4. How We Store Your Data

4.1 Current Version — Storage Model

Health & activity data stays on your device. Workout logs, wellness entries, GPS tracks, and nutrition data are stored exclusively in your device's local storage (or protected app storage when installed as a native app). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Usage analytics go to Hero Mode's servers. If you have opted in to analytics (the default), app usage events are sent to Google Firebase and stored in Hero Mode's Firebase project. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information — it is anonymous by design. You can stop this at any time via Settings → Data & Privacy → Usage Analytics.

4.2 Encryption at Rest

On-device data is protected by your operating system's built-in encryption:

  • iOS / iPadOS: Data is protected by iOS Data Protection (Class C) when your device is locked. This uses hardware-enforced AES-256 encryption tied to your device passcode.
  • Android: Data is protected by Android Full Disk Encryption (FDE) or File-Based Encryption (FBE) on devices running Android 6.0 and higher.
  • Desktop browsers: Data is stored in browser profile storage, protected by your operating system's file system permissions and, where available, OS-level encryption (e.g., macOS FileVault, Windows BitLocker).

We rely on OS-level encryption as the primary protection mechanism for stored health data. This is the same approach used by most consumer fitness apps and is legally defensible under GDPR, CCPA, and the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule for locally stored data.

4.3 Future Cloud Storage Phase

A future version of Hero Mode will introduce optional cloud backup to prevent data loss if you change devices. When that feature launches, data in transit will be protected by TLS 1.2 or higher, and data at rest on our servers will be encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent. This policy will be updated before that feature goes live. You will be asked for separate consent before any data is uploaded to our servers.

4.4 Data Residency

In the current on-device version, your data never leaves your device — data residency requirements do not apply. When cloud backup launches, data will be stored in the United States. We will establish appropriate legal mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for EU/UK users before enabling cross-border transfers.

5. How We Secure Your Data

We apply the following security measures appropriate to the current on-device architecture:

ControlDescription
REST Encryption at rest All data is stored in device local storage, protected by iOS Data Protection (AES-256, hardware-enforced) on Apple devices and Android FDE/FBE on Android devices. We rely on OS-level encryption rather than application-layer encryption for v1.
TRANSIT Encryption in transit No health data is transmitted in v1 (all data stays on device). Any future network requests (e.g., AI-generated recipes via Claude API) use HTTPS / TLS 1.2 or higher. No health data is included in these requests.
ACCESS App-level access control The App Lock feature (PIN protection) prevents unauthorized access to the app UI on a shared or unattended device. The PIN is stored as a hashed value; the plain PIN is never stored.
CONSENT Granular data consent On first launch, users explicitly consent to each health data category (workout, wellness, GPS, nutrition) individually, and separately to usage analytics. All preferences are recorded with a timestamp and can be changed at any time from Settings → Data & Privacy.
RETENTION Data retention limits Users can set a data retention window (6 months, 1 year, 2 years, or forever). GPS walk data is automatically purged after 12 months regardless of retention setting. Old records are removed on each app launch.
BREACH Incident response We maintain a written breach notification procedure compliant with the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 CFR Part 318). In the event of a breach affecting your health data, we will notify you within 60 days as required by federal law.

No storage mechanism is 100% secure. If your device is lost, stolen, or accessed by someone who knows your passcode, your on-device data may be at risk. We recommend enabling a strong device passcode and the Hero Mode App Lock PIN for additional protection.

7. Advertising

The free version of Hero Mode displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), IP address, and device signals to serve relevant ads.

On iOS, we will present an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before AdMob accesses your IDFA. You can deny tracking and still receive non-personalized ads. On Android, you can opt out of ad personalization in your device settings under Google → Ads.

Hero Mode Premium subscribers do not see ads and AdMob does not collect advertising identifiers from Premium users.

For more information on Google's data practices: policies.google.com/privacy.

8. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Hero Mode offers a Premium subscription. All payment processing is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We never see, receive, or store your payment card information.

We receive confirmation of your subscription status and an anonymized subscriber ID from the platform, which we use to unlock Premium features. Subscription management — including cancellation — is done through your Apple ID or Google account settings.
