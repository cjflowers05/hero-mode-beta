Privacy Policy

Hero Mode · Last updated: August 8, 2026 · Effective: August 8, 2026

Short version: Hero Mode stores all of your workout data locally on your device. We do not operate a backend server, do not collect personal information ourselves, and do not sell your data. The free tier includes ads served by Google AdMob, which has its own data practices described below.

1. Who We Are

Hero Mode is developed and published by Cody Flowers ("we," "us," "our"). For privacy questions, contact us at cody.flowers@mymail.champlain.edu.

2. Data We Collect and Store

Hero Mode stores the following data exclusively on your device using your device's local storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers (we have none) or sold to third parties.

  • Workout logs — exercise names, set counts, weight, and reps you record during sessions
  • Profile — the name you choose for your hero identity, selected theme, and XP/level progression
  • Training preferences — your weekly schedule, goals (muscle, strength, fat loss, endurance), and session settings
  • Nutrition check-ins — meal descriptions, water intake, and mood notes you optionally log
  • Session notes — free-text notes you write at the end of a session
  • App settings — theme, rest timer duration, unit system (lbs/kg), and notification preferences

You can delete all this data at any time by clearing the app's storage through your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation or email address
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts
  • We do not collect health data from Apple Health, Google Fit, or any health platform (future integration would require explicit opt-in and is not active)
  • We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites

4. Advertising (Free Tier)

The free version of Hero Mode displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads, AdMob may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • IP address (used to infer general location)
  • Device type, OS version, and app usage signals

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own Privacy Policy. You can review Google's data practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

On iOS, AdMob will ask for your App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before using your IDFA for personalized ads. You can deny this permission to receive non-personalized ads instead. On Android, you can opt out of ad personalization in your device settings under Google → Ads.

Hero Mode Premium subscribers do not see ads.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Hero Mode offers an optional Hero Mode Premium subscription. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We never see, store, or process your payment card information. Your subscription status is verified through the platform's receipt validation API.

Subscription management (cancel, upgrade, restore purchases) is handled through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

6. Push Notifications

If you opt in to push notifications, your device generates a push token that is sent to our notification service provider. This token is used only to deliver workout reminders and motivational messages you have configured. You can withdraw consent at any time through your device's notification settings for Hero Mode.

7. Workout Sharing

Hero Mode lets you share a workout image card using your device's native share sheet. The image is generated on your device; we do not upload it to any server. If you share it to a social platform, that platform's privacy policy governs what happens to the image.

The optional "import workout" feature encodes workout data as a URL parameter. No server is involved — the data travels directly in the URL between devices.

8. Children's Privacy

Hero Mode is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Data Security

Because all workout data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's built-in security (screen lock, device encryption). We have no access to this data and cannot retrieve it if your device is lost.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained on your device for as long as you have the app installed. To delete your data:

  • All data: Uninstall the app, or go to your device Settings → Hero Mode → Clear Storage
  • Specific logs: Use the in-app History tab to delete individual session records

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when we add new features (such as cloud sync or new third-party integrations). When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify you within the app on your next launch.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy laws to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full direct control over it. For any questions or requests, contact us at the address below.
